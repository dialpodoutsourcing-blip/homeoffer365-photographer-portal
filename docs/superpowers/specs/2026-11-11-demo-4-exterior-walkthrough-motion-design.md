# Demo 4 Exterior Walkthrough Motion Design

## Intent

Make Demo 4's exterior video feel like a person steadily walking around the property instead of viewing nearly stationary photographs.

## Scope

- Rebuild the exterior walkthrough from the existing Demo 4 exterior property images.
- Preserve the current property, logical exterior sequence, clean presentation, video placement, and controls.
- Use a smooth forward push from approximately `1.04` to `1.20` scale per scene, with restrained lateral movement and gentle crossfades.
- Replace only the exterior walkthrough MP4 and its poster. Keep the interior walkthrough and page layout unchanged.

## Approaches considered

1. Rebuild from source images (selected): produces stronger, cleaner motion without magnifying existing video compression.
2. Zoom the existing MP4: quicker, but compounds compression and may exaggerate existing movement.

## Success criteria

- Forward movement is clearly visible throughout the exterior tour.
- Motion remains smooth and comfortable, without abrupt crops, black edges, or distracting overlays.
- The rendered MP4 remains web-friendly and the site tests and production build pass.
