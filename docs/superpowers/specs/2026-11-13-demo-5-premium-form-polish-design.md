# Demo 5 Premium Form Polish Design

## Intent

Give Demo 5 a polished, premium mobile form without adding visual clutter. The form should feel calm, trustworthy, and purpose-built for professional photographers.

## Approved direction

Use an understated visual system: navy typography, restrained blue accents, warm-white surfaces, generous but efficient spacing, subtle dividers, and minimal shadows. Reduce nested-card appearance while keeping inputs, category tabs, and photo items visually distinct where interaction requires it.

Refine the form header, step progress, section headings, labels, fields, capture controls, checklist tabs, photo cards, notices, and navigation buttons as one consistent system. Preserve every field, label, validation rule, draft behavior, camera/upload behavior, accessibility semantic, and submission rule.

## Alternatives considered

- Understated premium (chosen): strongest fit for the brand and the request for fewer containers.
- Corporate dashboard: clearer boundaries but would reintroduce too many boxes.
- High-contrast modern: visually bold but less calm and professional for field use.

## Constraints

- No new dependency, image, database change, or workflow change.
- No extra containers for decoration.
- Must remain usable without overflow at 320px and 390px.
- Touch targets remain at least 44px.
