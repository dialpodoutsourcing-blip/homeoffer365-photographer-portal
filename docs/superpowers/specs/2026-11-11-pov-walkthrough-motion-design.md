# POV Walkthrough Motion Design

## Intent

Make both Demo 4 walkthroughs feel recorded by an unseen person walking at chest height, not like slow zooming photographs.

## Design

- Keep each tour at approximately one minute while moving continuously through more of the property.
- Use linear forward travel with a stronger scale range, plus restrained step-frequency vertical bob and lateral sway.
- Keep turns and crossfades short and smooth.
- Interior route must visit entry, living room, dining area, kitchen, hallway, both bedrooms, bathroom, and laundry/utility.
- Exterior route must cover the full home perimeter, fence/boundary, rear, AC condenser, electrical service, and meter.
- Never show a person, hands, body, shadow, reflection, camera rig, captions, or overlays.

## Success criteria

- Motion stays active through the full duration instead of easing nearly to a stop.
- Every required interior and exterior stop appears.
- No blank edges, abrupt jumps, or visible operator artifacts.
