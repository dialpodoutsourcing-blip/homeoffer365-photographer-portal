# Demo 4 Content and Gallery Refresh

## Intent

Make Demo 4 cleaner and more useful on mobile: move the logo into the hero, remove redundant calls to action, simplify team and review copy, and turn sample categories into compact product-style galleries.

## Decisions

- The official house logo sits at the hero's upper-right red-X position; the separate top header is removed.
- The hero submit button and floating camera button are removed.
- The entire one-line team strip links to the team page; it has no secondary button.
- Review eyebrow copy becomes `VERIFIED VENDOR FEEDBACK`; the Google Reviews link is removed.
- Review names align with the existing portraits: Michael Davis, Emily Carter, Daniel Wilson, Jessica Miller, and Sarah Thompson.
- Exterior, Interior, and Mechanicals each use one large selected image with several clickable thumbnails.
- Street View is embedded in the page rather than opening Google Maps.
- The Videos tab is renamed `Video Tours` and retains the working full-property video.

## Constraints

- Keep the page compact and responsive.
- Preserve keyboard-accessible tabs and thumbnail controls.
- Do not modify Demo 1-3.
